# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different experience, backgrounds, and abilities. Critique implementations and evidence without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Apply restrictions consistently and explain decisions privately to the people involved where safe.

Use the private reporting contact published by repository maintainers to raise conduct concerns. That contact must be configured before public community participation begins.
